1. Introduction

This Cookies Policy explains how our Project Management System uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our application.

Cookies help us provide a secure, reliable, and convenient experience for users.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your web browser when you visit a website or web application. They allow the application to remember certain information and maintain your session between requests.

3. How We Use Cookies

Our application may use cookies for purposes such as:

  • Keeping users securely authenticated
  • Maintaining user sessions
  • Protecting authenticated requests
  • Remembering necessary application settings
  • Improving application reliability and performance

4. Authentication Cookies

Our application may use an authentication cookie to maintain your logged-in session. This cookie helps the server recognize authenticated requests and protect access to private features.

Authentication cookies are used for security and application functionality rather than for advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

Some services used by the application may use their own technologies to provide specific functionality. For example, email delivery services may process information required to deliver messages.

We do not use cookies for selling personal information or displaying targeted advertising within the application.

6. Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings. However, disabling necessary authentication cookies may affect your ability to log in or use protected features of the application.

7. Essential Cookies

Some cookies may be essential for the application to function correctly. These cookies support authentication, security, and session management and cannot be disabled while using certain protected features.

8. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy when our application, technologies, or practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us
